   Norm-style broadcast computation. This pattern typically takes              References
the form C = A[..., C] * Scale[C], where the channel dimension C                   TBD
is often 128/256/512/768. With VLEN=1024, 16 vector registers are

   FlashAttention. In FlashAttention, the hidden dimension is com-

allocation, code that heavily uses LMUL=4 or LMUL=8—especially
in transcendental-function approximations such as exp and tanh—is

provide a compute width equivalent to VLEN=256 with LMUL=4,
substantially reducing the probability of vector-register spills and
improving kernel robustness.
